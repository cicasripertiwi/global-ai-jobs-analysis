# Global AI and Data Science Job Market Analysis

## Project Overview
This project explores the global AI and Data Science job market through data analysis and visualization. It focuses on salary trends, job opportunities, and factors that may influence salaries across different countries, job roles, and industries.

## Objectives
- Analyze average salaries across different job roles.
- Compare salaries between countries.
- Explore salary differences based on experience levels.
- Examine salary trends by company size and work mode.
- Compare salaries across AI specializations and industries.
- Identify countries with the highest number of job openings.

## Dataset
The dataset contains:
- **90,000 rows**
- **35 columns**

It includes information about job roles, salaries, countries, AI specializations, experience levels, industries, company sizes, and employment conditions.

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab

## Analysis Performed
1. Average Salary by Job Role
2. Average Salary by Country
3. Average Salary by Experience Level
4. Average Salary by Company Size
5. Average Salary by Work Mode
6. Average Salary by AI Specialization
7. Average Salary by Industry
8. Job Openings by Country

## Key Findings
- Research Scientist has the highest average salary among the analyzed job roles.
- The USA has the highest average salary among the countries in the dataset.
- Lead-level employees have the highest average salary.
- Salary differences across company sizes are relatively small.
- Remote, Hybrid, and On-site work modes have similar average salaries.
- Generative AI has the highest average salary among the analyzed AI specializations.
- Average salaries across industries are relatively similar.
- Singapore has the highest number of job openings in the dataset.
